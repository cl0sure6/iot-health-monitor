About the System

MetaSense IoT is a healthcare monitoring system for patients living with metabolic syndrome — the combination of high blood glucose, high blood pressure, excess waist fat and abnormal cholesterol that raises the risk of diabetes and heart disease.

A wearable band and a small chest patch collect body temperature, blood glucose, heart rate, blood pressure, blood oxygen and a single-lead ECG. The devices send their readings to a gateway over Wi-Fi every few minutes, the server compares each value with the range set by the attending doctor, and this dashboard shows the result. When a value leaves its range, the system raises an alert for the care team instead of waiting for the next appointment.

  • Continuous readings arrive around the clock, not once a month.
  • Automatic every value is checked against individual limits.
  • Shared the patient, the nurse and the doctor see the same numbers.

Alert Section

Examples of how the system reacts to normal and abnormal measurements.

Normal — Heart Rate 78 bpm

Inside the 60–100 bpm range. The reading is written to the patient record and no message is sent.

Warning — Blood Glucose 9.4 mmol/L

Above the 7.8 mmol/L limit two hours after a meal. The patient gets a reminder in the mobile application and the nurse sees a yellow marker next to the name in the ward list.

Critical — Blood Pressure 146/92 mmHg

Stage 1 hypertension confirmed by three readings in a row. The system calls the attending doctor and asks the patient to rest and measure again in 15 minutes.
